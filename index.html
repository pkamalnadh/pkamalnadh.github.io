05 / Product Thinking

From a problem to product impact.

A structured approach to thinking about product discovery and development.

01

Discover

Understand users, research challenges and validate the problem worth solving.

02

Define

Establish product goals, target users, value propositions and success measures.

03

Prototype

Explore solutions, design an MVP concept and test assumptions with users.

04

Improve

Use feedback and evidence to evaluate outcomes and refine the product.

Product Principle

Start with the problem, not the technology.

An AI product should solve a genuine problem, deliver a clear benefit and work reliably while respecting user privacy and safety.
