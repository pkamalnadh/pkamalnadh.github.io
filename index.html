05 / Product Thinking

From problem to product impact.

My developing approach to thinking through an AI product opportunity.

01

Discover

Understand users, identify pain points, interview potential users and validate the problem.

02

Define

Set the product goal, target audience, value proposition and measurable outcomes.

03

Design & Test

Develop an MVP concept, prototype key workflows and test assumptions with users.

04

Improve

Review feedback, reliability, user outcomes and business metrics to guide iterations.

My AI product principle

Start with the problem, not the technology.

An AI feature is valuable when it solves a genuine user problem reliably, responsibly and in a way that supports a clear product objective.
